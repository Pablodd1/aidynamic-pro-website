What is Zero-G Simulation?

A revolutionary approach to market testing that replaces guesswork with data.

5,000 Unique AI Personas

Each persona is a fully realized AI agent with distinct demographics, behaviors, preferences, income levels, pain points, and decision patterns. They're not random — they're calibrated against real market data to mirror your actual target audience.

Example personas include: A 34-year-old marketing director in Miami who values efficiency over price. A 52-year-old small business owner who's skeptical of new technology. A 28-year-old startup founder who makes decisions in under 24 hours.

The simulation runs them through your entire funnel: discovery → consideration → objection handling → conversion — all in a compressed timeframe that delivers results in 48 hours.

Process

How It Works

Four steps from brief to actionable insights.

1

Define Your Scenario

You tell us what you want to test: a new product, pricing change, marketing message, or competitor response. We build a detailed simulation brief with your target demographics, market segment, and key questions to answer.

2

Persona Calibration

We generate and calibrate 5,000 AI personas against real market data. Each one gets a detailed profile: age, income, location, pain points, buying triggers, objections, and decision speed. This isn't random — it's statistically representative.

3

Run the Simulation

The personas interact with your offer in a realistic environment. They browse, compare, object, negotiate, and convert — or don't. Every interaction is logged. We run multiple scenarios: different prices, messages, features, and competitive responses.

4

Get Actionable Insights

You receive a comprehensive report with predicted conversion rates, optimal pricing, most effective messaging, common objections, and persona-specific insights. Know exactly what to change before you spend a dollar on real marketing.
